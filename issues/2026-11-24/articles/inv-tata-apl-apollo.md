HEADLINE: Tata Steel to acquire 26.4% in APL Apollo Tubes for $1.67 billion
PUBLISHED: Sun, November 22, 2026 at 9:43 PM CST
ATTRIBUTION: Roushni Nair, Investing.com
BODY (verbatim fragments, in page order):
Investing.com -- Tata Steel on Monday agreed to acquire a 26.38% stake in APL Apollo Tubes for $1.67 billion,
marking a major expansion of its downstream steel business
and triggering a mandatory offer that could give it majority control of India's largest structural steel tube manufacturer.
Tata Steel will acquire 73.86 million shares from S Gupta Holding Private Limited,
a member of APL Apollo's promoter group, for a cash consideration of about 155.1 billion rupees,
at 2,100 rupees per share.
The acquisition represents 26.38% of APL Apollo's expanded voting share capital on a fully diluted basis
and will be completed in three tranches,
with the final transfer expected by March 31, 2028,
subject to applicable conditions.
APL Apollo disclosed the share purchase agreement in a stock exchange filing on Monday.
The first tranche of shares is being acquired through a block deal,
while the remaining transfers are subject to conditions precedent,
including necessary regulatory approvals.
Separately,
Tata Steel has approved a mandatory open offer to acquire up to an additional 26% of APL Apollo
from public shareholders at 2,301.40 rupees per share.
If fully subscribed,
and assuming no other acquisitions or changes in share capital,
Tata Steel's ownership could rise to about 52.38%,
giving it majority control of the company.
The open offer price is higher than the 2,100 rupees per share being paid to S Gupta Holding.
The offer covers up to 72.81 million shares and carries a maximum consideration of about 167.56 billion rupees,
assuming full acceptance.
The stake acquisition comes alongside two linked asset-transfer transactions
under which APL Apollo will acquire Tata Steel's tubes business for up to 117 billion rupees
and its entire 100% holding in Tata Steel Colors for 163 billion rupees.
The combined value of these transfers amounts to up to 280 billion rupees, or about $3.02 billion.
The tubes business includes steel tube and pipe manufacturing facilities at Jamshedpur, Hosur, Khopoli and Sahibabad,
along with certain cold-rolling and surface-coated steel product facilities at Khopoli.
Tata Steel owns Tata Steel Colors directly and through its unit,
Tata Steel Downstream Products Ltd, with each holding 50%.
Tata Steel expects the acquisition of APL Apollo to strengthen its downstream platform,
increase its exposure to higher-margin value-added products
and raise downstream integration to about 53% from around 35%.
