HEADLINE: "Certain Oil Country Tubular Goods From India, the Republic of Korea, the Republic of Türkiye,"
"the Socialist Republic of Vietnam, and Ukraine: Continuation of Antidumping Duty Orders and Countervailing Duty Orders"

PUBLISHED: "10/22/2026"

ATTRIBUTION: "International Trade Administration" (agency listed as "Department of Commerce")

BODY:
SUMMARY
1. "As a result of the determinations by the U.S. Department of Commerce (Commerce) and the U.S. International Trade Commission"
2. "(ITC) that revocation of the antidumping duty (AD) orders and countervailing duty (CVD) orders"
3. "on certain oil country tubular goods (OCTG) from India, the Republic of Korea (Korea), the Republic of Türkiye (Türkiye),"
4. "the Socialist Republic of Vietnam (Vietnam), and Ukraine would likely lead to the continuation or recurrence of dumping"
5. "and countervailable subsidies and material injury to an industry in the United States,"
6. "Commerce is publishing a notice of continuation of these AD and CVD orders."

Background
1. "On September 10, 2014, Commerce published in the Federal Register the AD orders on OCTG from India, Korea,"
2. "Türkiye, and Vietnam."
3. "On September 10, 2014, Commerce published in the Federal Register the CVD orders on OCTG from India and Türkiye."
4. "On July 16, 2019, following the termination of the agreement that suspended the antidumping duty investigation"
5. "on OCTG from Ukraine, Commerce published the Ukraine Order in the Federal Register."
6. "On July 1, 2025, the ITC instituted, and Commerce initiated, the second sunset review of the Orders,"
7. "pursuant to section 751(c) of the Tariff Act of 1930, as amended (the Act)."
8. "As a result of its reviews, Commerce determined that revocation of the Orders would likely lead to the continuation"
9. "or recurrence of dumping and countervailable subsidies, and therefore, notified the ITC of the magnitude of the margins"
10. "of dumping and subsidy rates likely to prevail should the Orders be revoked."
11. "On September 29, 2026, the ITC published its determination, pursuant to sections 751(c) and 752(a) of the Act,"
12. "that revocation of the Orders would likely lead to continuation or recurrence of material injury"
13. "to an industry in the United States within a reasonably foreseeable time."

Continuation
1. "As a result of the determinations by Commerce and the ITC that revocation of the Orders would likely lead to"
2. "continuation or recurrence of dumping, countervailable subsidies, and material injury to an industry in the United States,"
3. "pursuant to section 751(d)(2) of the Act, Commerce hereby orders the continuation of the Orders."
4. "U.S. Customs and Border Protection will continue to collect AD and CVD cash deposits at the rates in effect"
5. "at the time of entry for all imports of subject merchandise."
6. "The effective date of the continuation of the Orders will be September 29, 2026."
7. "Pursuant to section 751(c)(2) of the Act and 19 CFR 351.218(c)(2), Commerce intends to initiate the next five-year reviews"
8. "of the Orders not later than 30 days prior to fifth anniversary of the date of the last determination by the ITC."
