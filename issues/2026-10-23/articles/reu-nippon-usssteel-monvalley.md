HEADLINE: Nippon Steel to spend $3 billion in U.S. Steel plant equipment upgrade, Nikkei reports

PUBLISHED: October 22, 2026 | 10:03 am

ATTRIBUTION: "Reuters" (byline). Reporting credit: "(Reporting by Nichiket Sunil in Bengaluru; Editing by Vijay Kishore)"

BODY:
"Nippon Steel (TYO: 5401) will invest about $3 billion in the U.S. Steel-operated Mon Valley works in Pennsylvania […]"
"to upgrade the plant's hot rolling facility, the Nikkei newspaper reported on Thursday."
"The move is part of the Japanese steelmaker's pledge to invest $11 billion in U.S. Steel by 2028,"
"[…] following its acquisition of the company in 2025."
"Nippon Steel did not immediately respond to a Reuters request for comment outside regular business hours."
"Mon Valley's hot rolling equipment, which heats slabs of iron from blast furnaces and rolls them into thinner steel,"
"[…] has been in operation for about 90 years, Nikkei added."
"The new equipment is set to enter operation in the second half of 2029, which would enable production of"
"[…] higher-quality steel, the report said."
"Nippon Steel raised its fiscal 2026 earnings forecast in August, lifted by stronger U.S. Steel earnings"
"[…] and a firm steel market."
