HEADLINE: Striking unions at Antofagasta’s Centinela copper mine reach preliminary deal, ending strike
PUBLISHED: Published Oct 16, 2026, 10:42 PM / Updated Oct 16, 2026, 10:48 PM
ATTRIBUTION: By Reuters (Investing.com 전재)
BODY:
SANTIAGO, Oct 16 (Reuters) - Workers from two striking unions at Antofagasta’s Centinela copper mine in Chile reached a preliminary agreement for a new collective contract agreement, agreeing to end a strike that has lasted more than a week at the mine, the union said late on Friday in a statement sent to its members.

The unions called a meeting on Friday night to present the agreement to members, which will then be put to a ratifying vote on Saturday.
