HEADLINE: Codelco calls shareholders’ meeting to discuss over-inflated output
PUBLISHED: October 14, 2026 | 2:58 pm
ATTRIBUTION: Reuters (MINING.COM /web/ 전재) — (Reporting by Daina Beth Solomon, Editing by Iñigo Alexander)
BODY:
Chile’s state-run copper miner Codelco said on Wednesday it has called a shareholders’ meeting for November 5 to discuss its over-reported production figures for 2024 and 2025.

During the meeting, Codelco’s board will discuss recent press accounts of the matter, outline measures taken to protect company interests and review potential legal, regulatory and financial risks.
