HEADLINE: EU chems trade surplus slides in August, remains strongest segment
PUBLISHED: 16-Oct-2026
ATTRIBUTION: Morgan Condon (ICIS)
BODY:
LONDON (ICIS)–The trade surplus for Europe’s chemicals industry narrowed in August compared to a year prior, according to data released by Eurostat on Friday.

The European chemicals trade balance was recorded at €14.1 billion, down from €15.1 billion.

Despite the decline, the chemicals industry remained the strongest segment listed by Eurostat, retaining a double-digit surplus, while others fell into contraction.

The overall trade balance in August for the EU fell to -€25.2 billion, falling further into contraction from -€7.5 billion in August 2025.

Energy remained a key driver falling to €31.4 billion from -€24.3 billion in the same period.

Imports into the EU rose 18.2% on the previous year, at €228.0 billion from €192.9 billion.

Although exports also rose 9.4% to €202.9 billion from €185.4 billion, this was not enough to offset the value of coming into the bloc.

Exports outpaced imports into the period between January to August 2026 compared to the previous year.

Exports rose 0.4%, while imports gained 7.7% on the previous year.

China remained the EU’s largest trading partner, with the trade deficit falling to -€38.4 billion in August from -€30.1 billion the previous year, driven by a nearly 20% increase of imports to the bloc.

Eurostat data is subject to revision.
