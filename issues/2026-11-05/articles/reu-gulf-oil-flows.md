HEADLINE: Gulf oil flows dip in October from post-Iran war high, data shows
PUBLISHED: Tue, 3 November 2026 at 7:39 am GMT-8
ATTRIBUTION: By Ahmad Ghaddar (Reuters)
BODY:
LONDON, Nov 3 - Gulf oil exports dipped in October from September's levels, which were the highest since the Iran war started

Faced with intensifying attacks on shipping around the Strait of Hormuz, which reached a record in October,

Flows of crude, condensate and refined products, excluding LPG, averaged 17.1 million barrels per day in October

Data from another company, Kpler, also showed a drop in shipments, with crude and condensate flows down about 1 million bpd

Kpler also said an upward revision was likely.

Vortexa sharply revised up the September figure compared with the data it made available to Reuters in early October

Iraq, Oman and Kuwait all exported less, the figures showed.
