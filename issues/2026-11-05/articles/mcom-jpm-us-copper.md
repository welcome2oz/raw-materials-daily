HEADLINE: JPMorgan sees US copper output more than doubling
PUBLISHED: November 3, 2026 | 9:33 am
ATTRIBUTION: Staff Writer, MINING.COM (page metadata author: Cecilia Jamasmie)
BODY:
The investment bank estimates the country could add 1.3 million tonnes of annual copper capacity

However, most of that production is unlikely to come online until after 2030.

US copper output has halved over the past 30 years to roughly 1 million tonnes annually

The country’s share of global production has plunged from about 50% in 1900 to less than 5% today.

At full production, it could meet as much as 25% of US copper demand.

However, Resolution still faces permitting requirements and technical studies, with production unlikely to begin before 2030.

JPMorgan estimates US copper projects require about $28,000 in capital investment for each tonne

compared with a global average of $26,000.

JPMorgan identifies Freeport-McMoRan (NYSE), the largest US copper producer

The bank estimates Freeport has more than 250,000 tonnes of potential annual production growth
