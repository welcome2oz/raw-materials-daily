PUBLISHED: Published Nov 27, 2026, 11:36 AM
ATTRIBUTION: By Olesya Astakhova, Ahmad Ghaddar and Alex Lawler (Reuters)
BODY:
Iraq is dissatisfied with preliminary findings that assessed its production capacity at 4.9 million barrels per day.
