HEADLINE: Oil flows through Middle East chokepoints increased in the third quarter
PUBLISHED: November 19, 2026
ATTRIBUTION: EIA Staff (U.S. Energy Information Administration, Today in Energy)

QUOTES:
"Collective oil shipments through key chokepoints around the Middle East increased by 19%"
"in the third quarter of 2026 (3Q26)"
"according to our recently published Global Energy Security Data."
"Oil shipments through the Strait of Hormuz and Suez Canal each increased"
"while those through the Bab el-Mandeb decreased compared with the second quarter"
"when flows were severely constrained following the onset of the conflict with Iran."
"Flows around the Middle East increased because of the use of smaller ships to move oil out of the Persian Gulf"
"for ship-to-ship transfers of crude oil onto VLCCs, and producers have maximized utilization of infrastructure"
"to bypass the areas of highest risk."
"Initial shipping data from Vortexa show that shipments remained close to 3Q26 levels in October."
"We estimate that countries around the Middle East produced 23.1 million barrels per day (b/d) of crude oil in October,"
"the most since the beginning of the conflict."
"We estimate that global oil inventories were 400 million barrels lower at the end of October"
"than they were at the beginning of April."
"The world consumed an estimated 102.8 million b/d of liquid fuels in October,"
"up 2.8 million b/d from the 2Q26 average."
"In addition, shipping data from Vortexa show China imported 76% (4.7 million b/d) more crude oil and condensates"
"in October than it did in 2Q26."
"Supply to the market from the U.S. Strategic Petroleum Reserve (SPR) peaked in 2Q26 at more than 1 million b/d."
"However, those draws slowed to almost zero during October."
"Given the recent increase in exports and production from the Middle East, we estimate global oil inventories have begun to increase."
"On September 29, the U.S. Department of Energy announced an exchange that would offer 40 million barrels of crude oil"
"from the U.S. SPR for delivery to market in November and December."
"On October 15, the IEA announced it would deliver remaining committed strategic volumes to market."
