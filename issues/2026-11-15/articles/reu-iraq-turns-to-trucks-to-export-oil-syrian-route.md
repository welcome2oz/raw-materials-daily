HEADLINE: Exclusive-Iraq turns to trucks to export oil in overland Syrian route revival
PUBLISHED: Published Nov 13, 2026, 08:27 AM
ATTRIBUTION: By Reuters (byline on page: "By Aref Mohammed and Ahmed Rasheed"; dateline: "BASRA, Iraq, Nov 13 (Reuters) -")

QUOTES:
"Outside a depot near Basra, hundreds of tanker trucks queue to load Iraqi crude oil bound for the Syrian port of Baniyas"
"Iraq, OPEC’s second-largest producer, has been working to find alternative export routes for its oil"
"since the Strait of Hormuz, its usual outlet, was disrupted by the Iran war."
"Despite higher transportation costs, the overland route has also regained appeal"
"because of an end to years of conflict in Syria"
"the Syrian route has become a small but strategically significant alternative for moving cargoes of heavy crude."
"Since last month, Qatari energy group UCC Holding has been shipping Iraqi crude oil by truck to Baniyas"
"UCC began shipping 120,000 to 150,000 barrels per day (bpd) under a contract with state oil marketer SOMO"
"with cargo volumes depending on truck availability, Abdul Karim added."
"UCC already has a fleet of 6,000 trucks which it is expanding to 10,000 for the route"
"adding that about 150,000 barrels are unloaded per day."
"Since the deal took effect on October 26, UCC has loaded about 1.6 million barrels"
"UCC plans to load between 5 million and 14 million barrels of heavy crude over three months from October 14"
"at a discount of $38 per barrel, a document reviewed by Reuters showed."
"UCC is entitled to about $12 per barrel to cover trucking and related transportation costs through Syria under the deal."
"The oil is loaded onto ships in Baniyas for delivery to Mediterranean refineries in Greece, Italy and Turkey"
"Iraq produced nearly 4.5 million bpd in October, about half a million barrels more than a month earlier"
