HEADLINE: BASF ends pursuit of Evonik after two offers rejected
PUBLISHED: Published Nov 19, 2026, 16:34
ATTRIBUTION: Louis Juricic, Investing.com
BODY (verbatim excerpts, in page order; not the full body):
Investing.com -- BASF SE has ended its efforts to acquire Evonik Industries AG
after the specialty chemicals company rejected two non-binding, all-cash offers
saying a higher price would not be in its shareholders’ best interests.

BASF said it had indicated a willingness to consider a further moderate increase to its offer
but had not received meaningful engagement from Evonik’s Executive Board.

We are convinced that BASF’s derisked all-cash offer of 24 euros per share
would have represented significant additional value for Evonik shareholders,

The statement did not disclose the terms of both rejected proposals

It also did not include a response from Evonik.
