HEADLINE: Pemex crude oil exports fall 45% in August amid refining push

PUBLISHED: Sep 30, 2026, 01:26 PM

ATTRIBUTION: Editor Jaiveer Shekhawat; Investing.com

BODY:

Petroleos Mexicanos reported a 45% decline in crude oil exports for August compared to the same month last year, with shipments dropping to 275,737 barrels per day. The state-owned energy company released the data on Wednesday.

The export decline came as Pemex increased its domestic refining operations. The company processed 1.2 million barrels per day at its six refineries, marking a 15% increase from August 2025.

Total crude and condensate production fell 2.2% from July to 1.64 million barrels per day in August. The production decline occurred despite higher global energy prices.

The Olmeca refinery contributed significantly to the refining increase, processing 252,471 barrels per day. This represents nearly three-quarters of the facility's 340,000 barrel per day capacity.

Gasoline production rose 5% from July to 439,346 barrels per day, while diesel output increased 9% to 316,279 barrels per day. The higher domestic fuel production allowed Pemex to reduce gasoline imports by 24% and diesel imports by 26%.

Fuel oil output decreased 30% to 174,416 barrels per day during the period.

The operational changes align with President Claudia Sheinbaum's energy sovereignty initiative, which seeks to eliminate import dependence by reducing crude exports and increasing domestic fuel processing.
