HEADLINE: Chile signals slow copper recovery from historic output slump
PUBLISHED: October 30, 2026 | 12:40 pm
ATTRIBUTION: Bloomberg News; (By James Attwood)
QUOTES:
"Chile expects its copper output to stage only a modest recovery next year after a historic slump"
"State copper commission Cochilco cut its 2027 production forecast to 5.08 million metric tons, implying a 3% rebound."
"It also slashed this year’s estimate to 4.94 million tons from 5.27 million amid accidents and wild weather"
"Chile’s output sank to its lowest quarterly level in at least two decades in the three months through September."
"Mines run by Codelco and BHP Group account for 77% of the net decline, according to Cochilco"
"Cochilco sees worldwide production recovering 3.4% in 2027 after contracting 0.6% this year"
"Chile and the Democratic Republic of Congo together accounting for about 37% of the increase."
"It forecasts a surplus next year equivalent to just 0.8% of consumption, leaving little cushion"
"Demand for refined copper is expected to grow 1.5% this year and 1.8% in 2027"
"Cochilco raised its copper-price forecasts to $6.18 a pound for 2026 and $5.95 for 2027, from $5.95 and $5.10 previously."
