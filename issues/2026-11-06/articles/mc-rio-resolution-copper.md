HEADLINE: Rio Tinto injects $144 million into Resolution Copper after land battle win
PUBLISHED: November 5, 2026 | 7:23 am
ATTRIBUTION: Cecilia Jamasmie | MINING.COM
BODY:
Rio Tinto (ASX, LON: RIO) has approved a $144 million investment

Rio Tinto will contribute $79 million, reflecting its 55% stake in the joint venture

BHP (ASX: BHP) will cover the remaining 45%

The companies expect the next phase to create about 50 additional jobs.

Resolution Copper has the potential to supply up to 25% of America’s copper demand for decades

The decision follows a lengthy legal battle over the transfer of federal land needed for the mine.

Located near Superior, Arizona, the copper deposit lies between 5,000 and 7,000 feet

Rio Tinto has targeted the mid-2030s for the mine’s opening

The project is estimated to contain more than 40 billion lb. (18.1 million tonnes) of copper

The latest commitment builds on about $110 million in contracts awarded by Resolution Copper in August

The joint venture estimates that Resolution Copper could generate thousands of jobs

contribute about $1 billion annually to Arizona’s economy once operational.
