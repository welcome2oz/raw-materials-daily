HEADLINE: Lundin cuts copper outlook again as wild Chile weather bites
PUBLISHED: November 5, 2026 | 5:57 am
ATTRIBUTION: Cecilia Jamasmie | MINING.COM
BODY:
Lundin Mining (TSX: LUN) has lowered its 2026 copper production forecast

The Canadian miner cut Caserones’ full-year guidance to 105,000–115,000 tonnes from 120,000–130,000 tonnes

Severe winter storms suspended operations at Caserones for about 30 days during the third quarter

Lundin produced 62,610 tonnes of copper in the three months ended Sept. 30

Gold production reached 36,763 oz., exceeding BMO’s 34,900-oz. forecast

Despite the weaker output, Lundin maintained Caserones’ cash cost guidance at $2.15–$2.35 per lb.

Record realized copper prices helped Lundin Mining generate more than $1.1 billion in quarterly revenue

A second phase would expand capacity to 40,000 tonnes through rectifier upgrades.

At Chapada, construction of an additional ball mill is about 15% complete, with commissioning targeted for late 2027.

The venture secured a long-term agreement with Argentina’s San Juan province

Lundin is targeting a sanction decision before year-end.
