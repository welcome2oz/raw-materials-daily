Commerce is publishing a notice of continuation of these AD and CVD orders.
Applicable September 23, 2026.
The merchandise covered by the scope of the orders is standard steel welded wire mesh from Mexico.
Dated: October 19, 2026.
Subject wire mesh may be packaged and sold in rolls or in sheets.
Any product imported, sold, or invoiced in one of these size combinations is within the scope.
