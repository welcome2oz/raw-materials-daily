HEADLINE: Thailand's PTTGC, SCGC expect to finalize JV details by Oct 2026

PUBLISHED: 01-Oct-2026

ATTRIBUTION: Jonathan Yee

BODY: 

SINGAPORE (ICIS)–Thailand's PTT Global Chemical (PTTGC) and SCG Chemicals (SCGC) are advancing talks for a proposed joint venture (JV) to combine their olefins and polyolefins businesses, the companies said in separate statements.

The parties have agreed on the assets that would be included in the JV and will finalize "key transaction parameters", including proposed shareholding structure, transaction steps and quantified synergies, within October 2026, per statements from PTTGC CEO Narongsak Jivakanun and SCGC parent Siam Cement's president and CEO Thammasak Sethaudom dated 30 September.

PTTGC and SCGC will then be submitting an application to the Trade Competition Commission of Thailand (TCCT) for approval.

The proposed joint venture would comprise PTTGC's crackers and polyethylene (PE) production facilities in Thailand, as well as its investment in subsidiary HMC Polymers, the firms said.

SCGC, meanwhile, is expected to contribute its Thai crackers, PE and polypropylene (PP) plants, along with investments in its subsidiaries Siam Polyethylene, Siam Synthetic Latex, Thai MMA and Bangkok Synthetics.

Under the transaction framework being considered, PTTGC is expected to become a major shareholder in the venture, while SCGC would remain a "significant shareholder". The companies did not disclose their prospective ownership stakes.

"The Potential Joint Venture remains subject to the internal approvals of the Companies, the approval of any relevant joint venture partners, if applicable, and the necessary approvals from the relevant regulatory authorities," the companies said.

PTTGC and SCGC said the combination was intended to create a leading regional petrochemical producer and strengthen Thailand's competitiveness in the industry.

The companies expect greater scale and a more integrated value chain to improve operational efficiency, supported by their feedstock position and downstream capabilities in higher-value and differentiated products.

The principal synergies are expected to come from operational optimization across the combined businesses, they said.

PTTGC and SCGC signed a non-binding memorandum of understanding in April 2026 to explore the combination of their Thai olefins and polyolefins operations, which included an agreement to conclude a study on the potential JV by the end of September.
