# HEADLINE
India reduces windfall taxes on diesel and jet fuel exports

# PUBLISHED
Sep 30, 2026, 01:28 PM

# ATTRIBUTION
Editor Jaiveer Shekhawat, Investing.com

# BODY

India lowered windfall taxes on diesel and aviation turbine fuel exports starting October 1, according to a government order released today.

The export duty on diesel fell to 16 rupees per litre from 20 rupees per litre. The levy on aviation turbine fuel exports dropped to 10.5 rupees per litre from 15 rupees per litre.

The export duty on petroleum products remained at 0.5 rupees per litre.
