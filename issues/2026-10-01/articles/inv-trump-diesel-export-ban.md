# HEADLINE
Trump weighs diesel export ban to contain U.S. fuel prices, FT reports

# PUBLISHED
Sep 30, 2026, 01:23 AM

# ATTRIBUTION
Ayushman Ojha, Investing.com (reporting on Financial Times)

# BODY

U.S. President Donald Trump is considering a range of measures, including a possible diesel export ban, to curb surging domestic fuel prices as a worsening energy crisis raises political pressure on his administration, the Financial Times reported on Wednesday.

Administration officials have discussed restricting international diesel sales and have briefed allies, including Britain, about possible supply disruptions, the FT reported, citing people familiar with the discussions.

A White House official reportedly said Trump was evaluating options to lower fuel prices but had made no decision on exports.

The debate has pitted Republican lawmakers from farming states against oil companies, with agricultural groups warning that high diesel prices could hurt farmers and industry ahead of the midterm elections.

U.S. diesel prices reached $6.53 a gallon last week, more than 70% above their prewar level, the FT reported.

Other measures under discussion include further Jones Act waivers, tax relief and easing rules governing lower-cost dyed diesel, the report added.
