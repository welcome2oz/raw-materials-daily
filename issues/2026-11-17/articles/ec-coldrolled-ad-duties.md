HEADLINE: Commission imposes anti-dumping duties on certain cold-rolled flat steel products
PUBLISHED: 16 November 2026
ATTRIBUTION: Directorate-General for Trade and Economic Security (Brussels)

QUOTES:
"The definitive anti-dumping duties imposed are 9.5% for India, 27.9% for Japan,"
"20.7-27% for Taiwan, 3-7.8% for Türkiye, and 16% for Vietnam"
"certain cold-rolled flat steel products from these countries were entering the EU at dumped prices"
"Cold-rolled flat steel is commonly used in most areas of manufacturing."
"The EU market for cold-rolled flat steel products is worth some €23.2 billion."
"The EU’s domestic production is based in 12 EU countries and employs over 8,600 people."
"The total value of imports into the EU amounts to €1,775 billion,"
"of which €282 million comes from India, €240 million from Türkiye, €180 million from Japan,"
"€345 million from Taiwan and €125 million from Vietnam."
"This probe is the first in which the Commission has established the existence of Particular Market Situations"
"A Particular Market Situation exists where conditions in an exporting country’s domestic market"
"In this investigation, the Commission established Particular Market Situations in India and Taiwan."
"In India, government measures affected the domestic price of iron ore, a raw material for cold-rolled flat steel."
"In Taiwan, Chinese overcapacity resulted in increased imports of cold-rolled flat steel from China"
"The investigation revealed the full magnitude of dumping on the EU market from these countries"
