HEADLINE: S Korea's LG Chem signs MoU with Volkswagen to develop plastics, composite materials

PUBLISHED: 28-Sep-2026

ATTRIBUTION: Jonathan Yee

BODY:

SINGAPORE (ICIS)–South Korean producer LG Chem has signed a memorandum of understanding (MoU) with Germany's Volkswagen Group to develop and expand the use of next-generation, environmentally friendly materials in automotive applications.

The companies will collaborate on plastics and composite materials aimed at reducing vehicle weight and improving performance, LG Chem said on Monday.

The companies will examine the use of acrylonitrile butadiene styrene (ABS) and engineering plastics (EP) in vehicle interior and exterior components.

ABS and EP are primarily used in the exterior components of home appliances and in automotive interior and exterior parts, LG Chem said.

The companies said they will conduct feasibility studies for specific applications and exchange information on technology and global market developments.

They will also explore expanding the use of post-consumer recycled (PCR) materials and cost-efficient material solutions across Volkswagen Group's automotive brands.

"We expect this to be a meaningful starting point for global automakers and materials companies to jointly drive innovation in future mobility materials," senior vice-president and head of LG Chem's petrochemical company Kim Sangmin said.
