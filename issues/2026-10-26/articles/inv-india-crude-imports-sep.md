"India’s crude oil imports increased 16% in September compared to August, reaching 22.26 million metric tons"
"The September imports were 9% higher than the 20.35 million tons imported in the same month last year"
"Oil product imports decreased 0.8% from August but fell about 40% compared to September last year."
"Product exports rose more than 6% on an annual basis to 5.22 million tons."
"The increase in imports has been largely driven by the return of Middle Eastern barrels and lower Russian volumes"
"said Giovanni Staunovo, an analyst at UBS."

"representing about one-third of total purchases, according to trade source data released Thursday."
"India’s fuel consumption in September increased 3.9% from August to 19.26 million metric tons, earlier data showed."
"LPG imports grew to 1.40 million tons in September from 1.30 million tons in August."
"Fuel oil imports dropped nearly 99% year-on-year to 0.01 million tons in September from 0.69 million tons a year earlier."
