HEADLINE: Rio Tinto to invest $1.9 billion in Queensland bauxite project
PUBLISHED: October 22, 2026 | 4:05 pm
ATTRIBUTION: Reuters
(Reporting by Rajasik Mukherjee in Bengaluru; Editing by Shreya Biswas)
BODY_QUOTES:
"Rio Tinto (ASX: RIO) said on Friday it will invest $1.9 billion to develop the Kangwinan bauxite project in Queensland"
"The miner said the investment would add 20 million tonnes of annual bauxite production in Weipa"
"with first output targeted in 2029 and full production expected by 2032."
"Kangwinan will replace production from the Andoom mine near Weipa as it reaches the end of its life this decade,"
"while increasing Weipa’s annual production capacity to about 44 million tonnes from around 38 million tonnes currently."
"The development will also help offset the planned 2029 closure of Rio’s Gove bauxite mine in the Northern Territory."
"The announcement follows Rio’s agreement last month to acquire the adjacent Aurukun Bauxite Project from Glencore"
"Rio said the combined Amrun, Kangwinan and Aurukun operations are expected to support its Weipa bauxite business"
