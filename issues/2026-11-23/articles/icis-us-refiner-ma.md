HEADLINE: INSIGHT: Surging fuel margins sparks US refiner M&A, investments
PUBLISHED: 20-Nov-2026
ATTRIBUTION: Al Greenwood, HOUSTON (ICIS)
BODY (verbatim excerpts, in page order; not the full body):
HOUSTON (ICIS)–In one month, an enormous amount of refining capacity changed hands as companies sought to capitalize
on what refiners expect will be higher margins for the rest of the decade.

In one deal, Valero is buying LyondellBasell’s Houston refinery
and plans to restart the light refining portion of the complex.

In another, Marathon Petroleum is selling two mid-continent refineries to CVR Energy

Marathon Petroleum will receive $3.4 billion of the proceeds of the sale

Valero plans to restart the the light crude processing portion of the Houston refinery by the end of 2027

Marathon Petroleum estimates that global outages reached 10 million bbl/day, which is nearly 43% above average.
