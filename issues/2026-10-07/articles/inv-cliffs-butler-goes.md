HEADLINE: Cleveland-Cliffs to host energy officials at steel facility

PUBLISHED: Oct 05, 2026, 04:20 PM

ATTRIBUTION: By Investing.com

BODY:

CLEVELAND - Cleveland-Cliffs Inc. (NYSE:CLF) will host U.S. Secretary of Energy Chris Wright and other federal officials at its Butler Works facility on Tuesday to showcase a grain oriented electrical steel expansion project, according to a press release statement.

The visit will highlight the company's $200 million investment in Butler Works, which includes a $75 million award from the U.S. Department of Energy. The project aims to expand the facility's grain oriented electrical steel production volume by up to 25 percent.

Grain oriented electrical steel is used in electrical transformers that support the nation's power grid. Cleveland-Cliffs stated it is the sole U.S. producer of this material.

The expansion includes installation of new induction reheat furnaces manufactured in Warren, Ohio, along with other infrastructure improvements. The upgrades are designed to enable more efficient heating of steel slabs, improve product quality, reduce production losses, and lower emissions from the hot rolling process.

Project completion is expected in early 2028. The company said the project is expected to support approximately 220 construction jobs at peak construction activity.

Butler Works employs approximately 1,200 people represented by United Auto Workers Local 3303. The facility generates approximately $187 million in annual wages and spends more than $200 million annually on local supplies and services, according to the company.

"This investment demonstrates the importance of maintaining domestic manufacturing capabilities for materials that are essential to America's energy infrastructure," said Lourenco Goncalves, Cleveland-Cliffs Chairman and CEO.

During Tuesday's visit, federal officials will receive an update on project progress and discuss the role of domestic grain oriented electrical steel production in supporting the electric grid.

Cleveland-Cliffs is headquartered in Cleveland, Ohio, and employs approximately 25,000 people across its operations in the United States and Canada.
