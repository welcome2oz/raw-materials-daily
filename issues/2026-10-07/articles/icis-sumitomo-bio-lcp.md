HEADLINE: Japan's Sumitomo begins production-scale manufacturing of bio-based LCP

PUBLISHED: 06-Oct-2026

ATTRIBUTION: Jonathan Yee (ICIS)

BODY:

SINGAPORE (ICIS)–Sumitomo Chemical has achieved production-scale manufacturing of liquid crystal polymer (LCP) containing biomass-derived monomers, advancing the material closer to mass production, the Japanese producer said on Tuesday.

The company said it is preparing a mass-production system for the bio-based LCP, and plans to begin providing customers with samples made through the production-scale process.

Based on an internal evaluation, the bio-based LCP has heat resistance, fluidity, and mechanical properties equivalent to conventional LCP made from fossil-based feedstock, according to the company.

The development follows Sumitomo's announcement in June 2025 that it had established mass-production technology for bio-based LCP.

At the time, the company said it aimed to obtain customer certification by the end of fiscal year 2026 and to begin supplying the product from fiscal year 2027.

LCP is a high-performance engineering plastic valued for its heat resistance, flame retardancy and fluidity, and is used in smartphones and other electronic devices, automotive components, and office automation equipment.
