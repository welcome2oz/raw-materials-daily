HEADLINE: European Commission picks 46 new strategic critical mineral projects
PUBLISHED: October 9, 2026 | 7:03 am
ATTRIBUTION: Reuters (Reporting by Julia Payne. Editing by Mark Potter)
EXCERPTS:
"The European Commission has chosen 46 strategic raw material projects to benefit from faster permitting"
"Beijing has increasingly used those dependencies as a trade lever over the last two years"
"Progress on the projects picked last year has been slow and some have struggled to move forward."
"The Commission said it had already mobilised more than €2 billion ($2.24 billion) in public funding"
"The projects are spread across 16 EU countries and cover 15 of the EU’s list of 17 strategic raw materials"
"The 46 new projects are expected to require around €21.1 billion ($23.7 billion) in capital investment"
"Notable projects include the Zinnwald Lithium project in Germany, the Skouries mine in Greece,"
"Arctial’s planned aluminium smelter in Finland and the Viscaria copper project in Sweden"
"Strategic projects do not automatically receive EU funding."
"Instead, the Commission said it “facilitates access” to EU funding"
"The latest set of projects follow 60 that were announced last year, including 13 outside the EU"
