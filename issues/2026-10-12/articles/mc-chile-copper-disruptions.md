HEADLINE: Chile copper mines disruptions put 5% of production at risk
PUBLISHED: October 8, 2026 | 3:56 am
ATTRIBUTION: Cecilia Jamasmie | MINING.COM
EXCERPTS:
"Strikes, severe weather, accidents and geological problems put an average 5.2% of production"
"GEM Mining Consulting’s first Production-at-Risk Index assessed 14 major copper operations across eight threats"
"El Teniente ranked highest at 9%, followed by Los Pelambres at 8% and Escondida at 7.5%."
"Centinela’s labour risk is also rated high, with more than 700 workers walking off the job on Oct. 7"
"A high rating represents an expected production loss of 3%, or roughly 11 days of annual output."
"Labour and metallurgy and grades are the biggest contributors to the index"
"Collahuasi’s metallurgy and grades risk is rated high after its ore grade fell to 0.90% from 1.15%,"
"Chuquicamata carries high safety risk after restrictions following an accident"
"Gabriela Mistral carries high metallurgy and grades risk after geological factors contributed to a 20% output decline."
"The index is an exposure measure rather than a forecast that 5.2% of production will necessarily disappear."
"At El Teniente, the exposure approaches one in 10."
