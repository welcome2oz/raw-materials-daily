HEADLINE: Germany to release 15 million barrels from oil reserves

PUBLISHED: Published Oct 09, 2026, 08:16 AM

ATTRIBUTION: Editor Vlad Schepkov; "By Investing.com" (meta byline); photo credit "© Reuters"

EXCERPTS:
"Germany will release up to 15 million barrels of oil and petroleum products, the economy ministry said Friday,"
"as part of a Group of Seven agreement to release 100 million barrels of diesel and crude oil from emergency reserves."
"Economy Minister Katherina Reiche told Reuters that diesel and heating oil will be released first"
"She said Germany will fully implement the G7 agreement."
"We must always have the entire supply chain in mind. Available crude oil alone is not enough,"
"The United States led a pressure campaign on the European Union to draw down emergency diesel inventories."
"The Trump administration warned that failure to act could lead to an American ban on diesel exports."
"Reiche said German refineries are the most important asset for security of energy supply."
