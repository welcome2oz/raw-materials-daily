HEADLINE: Fourteen Economies Join the United States in Signing a Joint Ministerial Statement to Address Global Excess Capacity and Production in Key Manufacturing Sectors

PUBLISHED: October 07, 2026

ATTRIBUTION: No byline is shown. The dateline reads "WASHINGTON", and the site name is "United States Trade Representative".

EXCERPTS:
"On the margins of the Organisation for Economic Co-operation and Development (OECD) Trade Committee"
"convened a meeting of senior officials of 14 economies"
"Building on the productive discussions at the G20 Trade Ministerial in Milwaukee, Wisconsin,"
"expresses our collective resolve to work together in new, dedicated sectoral platforms"
"calls on all countries to take steps to end the use of non-market policies and practices"
"The signatories include the Trade Ministers of Argentina, Australia, Canada, the European Union,"
"India, Italy, Japan, the Republic of Korea, Mexico, Poland, Türkiye, the United Kingdom, and the United States."
"Over the course of the U.S. G20 presidency, numerous economies raised instances of structural excess capacity"
"Left unchecked, these issues will continue to cripple domestic industries, displace local production,"
"The Trump Administration will continue to engage with our trading partners to defend our domestic industries,"
