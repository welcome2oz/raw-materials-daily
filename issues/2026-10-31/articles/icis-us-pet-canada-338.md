HEADLINE: US to impose 50% tariffs on PET imports from Canada on Friday
PUBLISHED: 29-Oct-2026
ATTRIBUTION: Al Greenwood (ICIS)

BODY (verbatim excerpts, in article order):
- "The US is on track to impose on Friday tariffs of 50% on imports of polyester from Canada under Section 338."
- "The US added polyethylene terephthalate (PET) and elastomeric polyurethanes to the imports covered by the new tariffs"
- "in a proclamation published on 23 October."
- "The Section 338 tariffs provide no exceptions for imports that comply with the nations’ trade deal,"
- "known as the US-Mexico-Canada Agreement (USMCA)."
- "The US also had removed some products from the tariff list, none of which were commodity plastics and chemicals."
- "A full list of the modifications to the Section 338 tariffs can be found"
- "In 2024, Canada exported 71.7 million kg of PET under HTS code 3907.61.00, which was worth nearly $128 million,"
- "according to the Canadian government."
- "In 2025, the figures were 64.0 million kg worth nearly $110.6 million"
- "The US has already imposed an initial batch of Section 338 tariffs on certain imports from Canada."
- "The rate for the initial Section 338 tariffs is 50%,"
- "and they do not provide exceptions for Canadian imports that comply with the USMCA."
- "The US is imposing the tariffs to address what it alleges are discriminatory trade practices"
- "against its exports of dairy products, alcoholic beverages and automobiles to Canada."
- "The dairy and alcohol actions cover similar imports from Canada."
- "However, the tariffs imposed to address Canada’s automobile trade policies are being imposed on products"
- "such as natural honey, tulip bulbs, hides, textiles and wood products."
- "The Gellert Global Group a food importing company, has sued the US government over the tariffs."
- "The case was filed in the US Court of International Trade (CIT), and the case number is 26-cv-04636."
