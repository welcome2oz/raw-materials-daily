HEADLINE: Rio Tinto begins restarting Oyu Tolgoi underground operations after seismic event
PUBLISHED: October 30, 2026 | 6:35 am
ATTRIBUTION: Reuters
(Reporting by Sameer Manekar & Rajasik Mukherjee in Bengaluru; Editing by Shreya Biswas and Maju Samuel)

BODY (verbatim excerpts, in article order):
- "Rio Tinto (ASX: RIO), (LON: RIO) on Friday said it had begun “progressively” restarting underground operations"
- "at its Oyu Tolgoi copper mine in Mongolia after completing safety reviews"
- "following a seismic event that temporarily halted mining activities."
- "The miner said a seismic event on October 29 triggered a localised rockfall"
- "in a temporary storage area adjacent to the production zone,"
- "causing ground vibrations but resulting in no structural damage to the underground cave or operating infrastructure."
- "Oyu Tolgoi is one of the world’s largest known copper and gold deposits"
- "and is central to Rio Tinto’s copper growth strategy."
- "The miner owns 66% of the project, with the Mongolian government holding the remainder."
- "The miner said three employees sustained injuries, and no fatalities were reported,"
- "with two of them continuing to receive treatment for non-life-threatening injuries."
- "Rio Tinto also flagged delays affecting all vehicles passing through the Ganqimaodu border crossing"
- "between China and Mongolia, which have reduced the availability of trucks"
- "to transport copper concentrate from Oyu Tolgoi."
