HEADLINE: Mixed reactions to UK’s proposed 65% out-of-quota PET safeguard duty
PUBLISHED: 30-Oct-2026
ATTRIBUTION: Matt Tudball (ICIS)

BODY (verbatim excerpts, in article order):
- "LONDON (ICIS)–The UK Trade Remedies Authority (TRA)’s proposed safeguarding measures on polyethylene terephthalate (PET)"
- "and recycled PET (rPET) have received mixed reactions from market participants following the announcement on 23 October."
- "Under the proposed measures, imports of PET and rPET with a viscosity of 74ml/g and higher will be subject to a 65% duty"
- "once the volumes imported pass a set quarterly quota as detailed below."
- "Interested parties and contributors can make submissions in response to the proposed measures"
- "by 23:59 GMT on 22 November 2026 via the TRA website."
- "Table L1: Provisional Tariff Rate Quotas (tonnes)"
- "European Union | 36,926.83 | 37,747.43"
- "Mexico | 2,571.70 | 2,628.85"
- "Indonesia | 1,779.59 | 1,819.13"
- "Vietnam | 1,475.27 | 1,508.06"
- "Residual | 4,826.57 | 4,933.82"
- "The TRA has published a list of exempt countries under its Developing Countries Exemption (DCE) list"
- "in Annex 2 of the Provisional Affirmative Determination (PAD)."
- "Both Indonesia and Vietnam have been removed from that list because the TRA found their imports to the UK"
- "exceeded the TRA’s threshold."
- "All countries that are not specifically listed in Table L1, or Annex 2 of the PAD, fall under the ‘Residual’ category."
- "This would include China, Turkey and Oman, which were among the top exporters to the UK from outside the EU."
- "Within the EU, Belgium, Germany, Lithuania and the Netherlands account for the largest shares of PET exports to the UK,"
- "according to data from the ICIS Supply and Demand Database."
- "Initial reaction from UK-based PET buyers and EU-based rPET flake and food-grade pellet (FGP) producers"
- "was shock at the size of the 65% out-of-quota duty, and quarterly quotas for non-EU countries/areas."
- "They believe the Q1 volumes could be sufficient to allow most, if not all, of their regular volumes"
- "to still be exported to the UK without exceeding the limit."
- "If the proposed safeguard measure is introduced, then arrangements for administering and monitoring quotas will begin to apply."
- "HM Revenue & Customs (HMRC) administers tariff quotas through the customs declaration process, with information on quota availability and balances made publicly available to traders."
- "Traders can check HMRC’s Integrated Online Tariff page for details on quotas."
- "This is likely to have a bigger impact on deep-sea cargoes given the transit times from locations such as Asia or the Middle East."
- "Several market participants expect UK interest in deep-sea imports to drop significantly as a result,"
- "because buyers or importers would not want to risk ordering material that could potentially be 65% more expensive"
- "once it arrives in the UK."
- "Several sources also mentioned the possibility of storing imports in bonded warehouses,"
- "releasing the material at the start of the following quarter to allow it to be sold within that quarter’s quota."
- "Tariffs and import duties only apply when goods are removed from a bonded warehouse"
- "and released into free circulation in the UK domestic market."
- "Goods not intended for the UK market can be stored in a bonded warehouse with their duty and import VAT suspended."
- "Goods can be stored to delay payment of duties and import VAT until later entry to free circulation in the UK market."
- "The measure would apply for no more than 200 days, or until a definitive measure is implemented, whichever comes first."
- "But for now it seems the majority of those impacted by the proposed duties are treating the developments"
- "as if they will come into effect in their current form,"
- "although this will not be confirmed until after the consultation period closes on 22 November."
