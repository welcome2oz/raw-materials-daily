HEADLINE: South Korea unveils $747 billion 10-year plan to drive green industrial growth
PUBLISHED: 07-Oct-2026
ATTRIBUTION: Jonathan Yee

BODY:
SINGAPORE (ICIS)–South Korea has unveiled a won (W) 1,000 trillion ($747 billion) green transformation strategy that includes measures to decarbonize the petrochemical and refining industries while developing new sources of economic growth.

The Korean Green Transformation (K-GX) strategy covers 2026-2035 and is intended to achieve carbon neutrality alongside sustainable economic growth, according to a joint government press release issued on Wednesday.

The programme comprises W200 trillion in fiscal spending, at least W790 trillion in climate finance provided through five state-backed financial institutions, and additional funding.

Separate private investment in 10 K-GX signature projects is expected to total W220 trillion.

South Korea plans to accelerate the decarbonization of five emissions-intensive industries: steel, petrochemicals, cement, semiconductors and displays, and refining.

For petrochemicals, the government will support the development of electrically heated naphtha cracking centre (NCC) technology and increase the use of cleaner fuels and feedstocks.

The measures are intended to reduce emissions while expanding production of environmentally friendly, higher-value products, the government said.

The broader strategy targets an increase in the share of bio-based and recycled feedstocks used in petrochemical production to 10% by 2035 from 0.5% in 2025.

These include bio-naphtha and pyrolysis oil derived from plastic waste as alternatives to conventional fossil-based naphtha.

In refining, the government plans to increase the production of sustainable aviation fuel (SAF) and biodiesel using bio-based and waste-derived feedstocks.

South Korea is targeting an SAF blending rate of 3-5% by 2030 and 7-10% by 2035.

The strategy also targets an increase in the recycling and use of plastic waste and end-of-life tyres from 10,000 tonnes in 2025 to 200,000 tonnes in 2030 and 500,000 tonnes in 2035. It separately calls for support for the use of pyrolysis oils derived from these materials in refining processes.

Support is also planned for hydrogen production infrastructure and the commercialization of carbon capture, utilization and storage (CCUS), one of 10 green industries selected for focused development.

The 10 green industries are electric vehicles (EVs), batteries, solar power, wind power, small modular reactors (SMRs), power equipment, power semiconductors, heat pumps, hydrogen and CCUS.

The government also plans to expand government subsidy programs to ensure that more than 70% of new vehicles registered in the country are all-electric or hydrogen fuel-cell EVs by 2035.

Meanwhile, the government plans to establish a framework for sovereign green bonds by the first half of 2027 and introduce transition financing for emissions-intensive industries.

"Through the Korean Green Transformation (K-GX) strategy, the government aims to go beyond responding to the climate crisis, comprehensively restructure the Korean economy and establish the country as a green manufacturing powerhouse," the government said.
