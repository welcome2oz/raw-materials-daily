HEADLINE: Ukraine says it struck Russian oil refinery as drone attacks intensify

PUBLISHED: Sep 26, 2026, 05:56 AM

ATTRIBUTION: Simon Mugo, Investing.com

BODY:

Investing.com -- Ukraine said it struck Russia's Ilsky oil refinery in the Black Sea region of Krasnodar overnight, setting the facility ablaze as both countries reported large-scale drone attacks, Bloomberg reported.

Ukraine's General Staff said Saturday that its forces hit the refinery, with Krasnodar Governor Veniamin Kondratiev separately reporting that drone attacks had affected two facilities in the region.

Three people were killed and two injured in the incident, according to the Krasnodar region's operational headquarters.

The Ilsky refinery is located about 850 kilometres southeast of Kyiv and has a design capacity of roughly 6.6 million tons annually. The independent facility has been targeted several times during Ukraine's campaign against Russian energy infrastructure.

Kyiv has increased attacks on Russian refineries as it seeks to disrupt the country's production and sale of petroleum products. Fuel supply disruptions have prompted Russian authorities to temporarily restrict most exports of gasoline, diesel and jet fuel.

Russia's defence ministry said its forces intercepted and destroyed 645 Ukrainian drones overnight. The ministry also said Russian forces attacked a data processing centre in Kyiv, a dry cargo vessel in the Black Sea and targets in Odesa and the surrounding region.

Ukraine's Air Force separately said Russia launched 173 drones overnight, including more than 50 jet-powered drones, with attacks continuing Saturday.

At least five people were injured in an attack on Kyiv early Saturday, according to Mayor Vitali Klitschko.

Bloomberg said it could not independently verify claims made by either side.

The latest strikes come amid continued pressure on energy infrastructure during the war, adding another potential source of disruption for fuel supplies as global petroleum markets contend with supply risks stemming from the conflict in the Middle East.
