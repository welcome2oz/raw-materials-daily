HEADLINE: BASF confirms it is in talks to acquire German specialty firm Evonik

PUBLISHED: 25-Sep-2026

ATTRIBUTION: Morgan Condon (ICIS)

BODY:

LONDON (ICIS)–BASF is in talks regarding a potential takeover of Evonik, the German major announced on Friday.

The chemicals bellwether confirmed that it is in exploratory talks with RAG-Stiftung and Evonik Industries AG following market speculation earlier in the week.

BASF stated that the course and outcome of these discussions remain open at this stage.

"As part of its corporate strategy, BASF continuously evaluates the strategic option of acquisitions that strengthen its core businesses, deliver a strong strategic fit, drive profitable growth and create value. In doing so, BASF takes a disciplined approach," BASF said in a statement.

The company stated it will inform the market in accordance with regulatory requirements without delay, if and to the extent required.

Evonik this week announced it was sharpening its growth strategy, securing financial leeway as part of its restructuring program. As part of its efficiency drive, Evonik is cutting 3,200 jobs worldwide, with around 2,150 of those based in Germany.
