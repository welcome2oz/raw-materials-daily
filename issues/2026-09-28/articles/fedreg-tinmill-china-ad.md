HEADLINE: Tin Mill Products From the People's Republic of China: Preliminary Affirmative Determination of Sales at Less Than Fair Value, Preliminary Affirmative Determination of Critical Circumstances and Extension of Provisional Measures

PUBLISHED: A Notice by the International Trade Administration on 09/21/2026

ATTRIBUTION: Enforcement and Compliance, International Trade Administration, Department of Commerce. (Federal Register)

BODY:

SUMMARY:

The U.S. Department of Commerce (Commerce) preliminarily determines that tin mill products from the People's Republic of China (China) are being, or are likely to be, sold in the United States at less than fair value (LTFV). The period of investigation (POI) is October 1, 2025, through March 31, 2026. Interested parties are invited to comment on this preliminary determination.

DATES:

Applicable September 21, 2026.

Commerce preliminarily determines that the following estimated weighted-average dumping margins exist:
