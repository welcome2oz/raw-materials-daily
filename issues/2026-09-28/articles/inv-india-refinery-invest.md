HEADLINE: UAE and Saudi Arabia eye India refinery investments, minister says

PUBLISHED: Sep 24, 2026, 10:45 AM

ATTRIBUTION: Editor Jaiveer Shekhawat, Investing.com

BODY:

Investing.com -- The United Arab Emirates and Saudi Arabia are interested in investing in India's refining sector, India's oil minister Hardeep Singh Puri said Thursday.

"Nobody wants to come and invest in a refinery unless they can also get a slice of your growing downstream market," Puri said at an event.

India, the world's third-biggest oil importer and consumer, plans to increase its refining capacity to 6.2-6.4 million barrels per day from the current approximately 5.4 million bpd through construction of new plants and upgrades to existing facilities, he said.

Puri noted that building a refinery requires substantial capital, approximately 780 billion to 800 billion rupees ($8.13-$8.34 billion).

"So yeah, they're very keen on these investments," he added.
