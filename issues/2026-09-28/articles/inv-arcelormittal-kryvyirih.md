HEADLINE: ArcelorMittal to halt Ukraine plant, flags $1 billion impairment after strikes

PUBLISHED: Sep 25, 2026, 04:19 AM

ATTRIBUTION: Editor Navamya Acharya; Investing.com

BODY:

ArcelorMittal said on Friday it will halt operations at its Ukrainian plant after four missile strikes in five weeks killed five workers and injured 17, triggering a roughly $1 billion non-cash impairment charge.

The steelmaker said it had told the Ukrainian government it could no longer safely restart ArcelorMittal Kryvyi Rih. The impairment mainly reflects damage to property, plant and equipment at the site.

The latest strike hit the plant on Sept. 21, following attacks on Aug. 17 and Sept. 14. One of the 17 injured employees remains in critical condition.

ArcelorMittal said it was discussing the site's future with the Ukrainian government and would preserve the infrastructure to keep open the option of restarting production when conditions allow.

The company has provided more than $700 million to its Kryvyi Rih subsidiary since Russia's invasion of Ukraine began in February 2022.
