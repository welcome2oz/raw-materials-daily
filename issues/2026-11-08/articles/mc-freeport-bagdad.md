HEADLINE: Freeport approves $4.5 billion Bagdad copper expansion
PUBLISHED: November 4, 2026 | 8:57 am
ATTRIBUTION: Frédéric Tomesco (MINING.COM)
BODY:
Freeport-McMoRan (NYSE: FCX) has approved a $4.5-billion expansion of its Bagdad mine in Arizona

to more than 400 million lb., an increase of about 225 million lb.

making Bagdad the second-largest copper mine in the United States behind Freeport…

Molybdenum output would climb by 12 million lb. annually to more than 20 million lb.

Initial production is targeted for mid-2030 following about three-and-a-half years of construction.

The project would add concentrating facilities capable of processing 115,000 tonnes of ore daily

Freeport anticipates a roughly 20% reduction in long-term unit net cash costs.

Capital costs are about $1 billion, or 29%, above the $3.5 billion estimate prepared in 2023.

Project economics are supported at a copper price of about $4 per lb., Freeport says.

China controls more than half of global copper smelting capacity, the White House says.

Net imports supplied 57% of US refined-copper consumption in 2025, according to data from the US Geological Survey.

Chile accounted for 68% of refined imports during 2021–24, followed by Canada at 16%.
