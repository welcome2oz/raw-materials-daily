# HEADLINE
Signs grow that US Trinseo will need more time to exit bankruptcy

# PUBLISHED
01-Oct-2026

# ATTRIBUTION
Al Greenwood

# BODY
HOUSTON (ICIS)– Trinseo's goal of emerging from bankruptcy protection by the end of 2026 is increasingly at risk as a dispute with dissenting creditors remains unresolved.

A complaint filed by those creditors remains pending before the bankruptcy court and could delay the company's restructuring process.

Those creditors did not support the company's pre-packaged reorganization plan. Instead, they filed a complaint alleging that some of the steps Trinseo took in 2023 to obtain more financing violated their credit agreement.

A judge continues to deliberate on the complaint after holding a trial in mid-August. A confirmation hearing on the company's pre-packaged reorganization plan was concurrently held, and that was also placed under advisement.

In the meantime, the judge instructed the two sides to start mediation to resolve their differences.

With mediation looming and the complaint pending, Trinseo asked the court to extend the time during which it can be the sole party to file a reorganization plan and to solicit support for the plan.

When Trinseo made the request, it argued that the exclusivity period was in danger of lapsing, which would allow the creditors to propose competing plans.

"A competing plan would introduce additional uncertainty, and potentially further delay any progress made during mediation," Trinseo said.

The company would like the court to extend the exclusivity period for filing a plan to 22 December from 23 September. For soliciting support for the plan, Trinseo would like the exclusivity period extended to 22 February from 23 November.

The requested extension of the solicitation deadline would push part of the restructuring timetable into 2027, raising the possibility that Trinseo could miss its earlier goal of emerging from bankruptcy protection before the end of 2026.

**BACKGROUND**
Trinseo had filed for bankruptcy protection under Chapter 11 on 26 May 2026. Under Chapter 11, Trinseo can continue operating while it is protected from certain creditors' actions.

Trinseo and a majority of its creditors had already reached an agreement on how to restructure the company's debt. Trinseo presented this prepackaged plan to the court shortly after filing for bankruptcy protection.

A minority group of creditors did not support the prepackaged deal and filed an adversary proceeding in bankruptcy court. That proceeding remains pending and will undergo mediation.

Trinseo filed for bankruptcy protection under Chapter 11 in US Bankruptcy Court, Southern Texas District. The case number is 26-90545.

The creditors filed their adversary proceeding in the same bankruptcy court. The case number is 26-03208.

Trinseo makes styrene butadiene latex (SB), acrylonitrile butadiene styrene (ABS), polymethyl methacrylate (PMMA) and other engineered materials. It is also a 50% partner in the AmSty styrenics joint venture, which makes styrene and polystyrene (PS).
