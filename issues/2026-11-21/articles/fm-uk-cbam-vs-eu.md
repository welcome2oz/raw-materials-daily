HEADLINE: UK CBAM to cost far less than EU’s for most importers, raising re-routing risk as ETS link nears
PUBLISHED: November 19, 2026 (meta article:published_time 2026-11-19T16:16:52+00:00 = 2026-11-20 01:16 KST)
ATTRIBUTION: By Fastmarkets Carbon Team (Fastmarkets)
BODY (verbatim excerpts, in page order; not the full body):
On default emissions values, a tonne of hot-rolled coil (HRC) from China could face a UK CBAM cost of about £34 in 2027.

Under the EU mechanism it could face about £177, a gap of roughly £143/t at Q3 2026 carbon prices.

HMRC published illustrative rates on 16 October, based on a Q3 UK ETS price of £58.15/t.

They are £9.69/tCO₂e for iron and steel, £9.79 for aluminium, £8.78 for cement and £1.45 for fertiliser.

Indian coated flat steel exports to the EU fell by around 30% in January-July 2026 compared with the same period of 2025.

The EU’s tighter steel import quotas, and the 50% duty on out-of-quota volumes, took effect on 1 July.
