HEADLINE: S Korea Lotte Chem swings to Q3 operating loss; YNCC integration in H1 2027
PUBLISHED: 11-Nov-2026
ATTRIBUTION: Jonathan Yee | Focus article by Jonathan Yee | SINGAPORE (ICIS)
BODY (verbatim excerpts, partial, article order):
SINGAPORE (ICIS)–South Korea's Lotte Chemical is moving ahead with restructuring its basic chemicals business
after posting a third-quarter (Q3) operating loss of won (W) 281.4 billion.
Lotte Chemical plans to form an integrated entity with YNCC in the first half of next year after spinning off its Yeosu plant
YNCC will also permanently shut two of its crackers, which have a combined ethylene capacity of 1.39 million tonnes/year.
Lotte Chemical said all production lines at the Yulchon compounding plant had started up in September.
The company recorded W110 billion operating profit in the second quarter.
Revenue rose 12.7% year on year in the third quarter to W5.40 trillion but fell 5.1% from the previous quarter.
Sales in the segment fell 5.5% quarter on quarter to W3.7 trillion.
Lotte Energy Materials narrowed its operating loss to W21 billion, from W34 billion a year earlier.
